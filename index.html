Questions

Good to know

Do my customers need an account or an app?

No. They get a link by email or text and open it on any phone or computer. They can choose options, accept, sign and download a PDF without signing up for anything.

Can I use my own contract?

Yes, and it works in every state. Upload your contract as a PDF in your company profile. When a customer accepts an estimate, they read your contract and sign it on the same page. BoltedBid adds a signature page with the customer, job address, estimate number and price, and keeps a signed copy for both of you. Your contract should meet your state's rules, so have your attorney review it.

What happens when my free trial ends?

You choose monthly ($15) or yearly ($130). Everything you created during the trial stays in your account.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month you've paid for, and you can download a backup of all your data first.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. BoltedBid works in any browser and installs on Android, iPhone and iPad like a regular app, including taking job photos with your camera.

I use Skynova now. Can I bring my work over?

Yes. Download your Skynova estimates and invoices as PDFs and BoltedBid reads them in, customers included.

Can customers pay online?

BoltedBid tracks deposits, milestone invoices and the payments you record, by check, cash, card or transfer. Taking card payments inside BoltedBid isn't available yet.
